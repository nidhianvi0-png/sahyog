How Sahyog Works

Three simple steps to start exchanging skills with people around you.

01

Create Your Profile

Sign up and list the skills you can teach and the skills you want to learn. No experience limit — everyone has something to offer.

02

Find Your Match

Our smart matching system connects you with someone whose skills complement yours. Teach what you know, learn what they know.

03

Exchange & Grow

Connect, schedule sessions, and start learning. Rate your experience and build a reputation as a trusted skill partner.

04

Earn Badges

Complete exchanges to earn achievement badges. Build your skill portfolio and showcase your learning journey.
